# External Integrations

- **Google Gemini API**:
    - **Model**: `gemini-2.0-flash`.
    - **Usage**: Nutritional breakdown, chatbot persona, hallucination correction, and multi-modal reasoning.
- **MongoDB Atlas**:
    - **Usage**: Cloud-hosted database for persisting user health profiles, meal history, and health logs.
- **Local Ingredient Database**:
    - **Format**: CSV/JSON.
    - **Usage**: High-performance $O(1)$ lookup for ingredient caloric density.
