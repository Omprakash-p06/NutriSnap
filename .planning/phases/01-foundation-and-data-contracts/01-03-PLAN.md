## Split Artifacts (`data/splits/`)

All split files are generated by `scripts/generate_splits.py` from `data/interim/dishes.csv`.
Splits are **grouped by dish_id** (all scans of one plate in the same partition) and
**stratified by calorie bins** `[0, 200, 400, 600, 800, 1000, 2000]`.

### train_ids.txt
One `dish_id` per line. ~70% of all dishes. Disjoint from `test_ids.txt`.

### val_ids.txt
One `dish_id` per line. ~15% of train dishes, carved out by GroupShuffleSplit.
Zero overlap with `train_ids.txt` or `test_ids.txt`.

### test_ids.txt
One `dish_id` per line. ~15% of all dishes. Held-out evaluation set.

### mvp_subset_ids.txt
One `dish_id` per line. 5–10 dishes from `train_ids.txt` selected to span the calorie
range (one per calorie quantile group). Used for MVP training and evaluation in v1.

### cv_folds.json

  "created": "YYYY-MM-DD",
  "random_seed": 42,
  "stratification": "calorie_bins",

  "folds": [
    {
      "fold_id": 0,
      "train_ids": ["dish_...", "dish_..."],

    }
  ]
}
