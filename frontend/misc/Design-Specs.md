# UI/UX Design Specifications - NutriSnap

## 1. Design Direction
The design implements a **Glassmorphism + Clay Morphism Hybrid**. 
- **Glassmorphism** is used for layout structures, data cards, and dashboards to create a premium, slightly frosted, transparent depth over vibrant photography.
- **Clay Morphism** is focused strictly on gamification elements: 3D soft badges, XP bars, achievement pop-ups, and interactive buttons that feel tactile, playful, and fun to push.

## 2. Color Palette: "Sunset Botanica"
Moving away from the overly common generic "food green", this palette utilizes warm, appetizing tones heavily inspired by sunset light and food photography, balanced with fresh premium accents.

- **Primary Gradient:**
  - Deep Coral (`#FF6B5A`) to Warm Amber (`#FFB347`)
  - *Usage: Primary buttons, core call-to-actions, active state elements.*
- **Fresh Accents:**
  - Sage Mint (`#3ECFA0`)
  - *Usage: Success states, subtle health accents, positive trend lines.*
- **Premium Accents:**
  - Deep Violet (`#6B3FA0`)
  - *Usage: XP progression, level milestones, luxury accents.*
- **Gamification Gold:**
  - Liquid Gold (`#F5C542`)
  - *Usage: Trophies, badges, streaks, leaderboards.*
- **Backgrounds:**
  - Dark Mode: Rich Night (`#12101F`) with warm, subtle under-glow elements.
  - Light Mode: Warm Ivory (`#FFF8F0`) with soft, diffused shadows.

## 3. Typography
- **Headings & Branding:** `Fredoka` (playful, welcoming, soft geometry).
- **Body & Data Rendering:** `Nunito` (highly legible, perfectly complements rounded elements).

## 4. Logo Concept
A clean, modern **geometric mark** logo representing a blend of nutrition, focus, and camera capture. Designed with the Sunset Botanica gradient and Sage Mint highlights with subtle glassmorphism styling.

## 5. UI Layouts & Core Screens
### 5.1. Daily Dashboard (Home)
Highlights the primary call to action (Camera button in the center). The top features a frosted glass card detailing daily Macros and a circular progress ring. Below, an XP bar and an achievements dock leverage soft-3D Clay Morphism.

### 5.2. Scan & Results Interface
Taking a picture displays a scanning animation. AI estimates appear rapidly with local models, then refine. Nutrient values are displayed in visually distinct Glassmorphic pills, while suggested meal alternatives peek up in a drawer.

## 6. Micro-Interactions
- Bouncy, playful feedback on button clicks (Clay Morphism push effect).
- Confetti and badge reveals with 3D rotations when leveling up.
- Smooth page transitions and skeleton loaders mimicking the Sunset Botanica gradients.
